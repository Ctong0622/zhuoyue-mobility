# Client Feedback

## Client
Liming

## Feedback

After reviewing the website, Liming said that the overall website and information looked very good. He requested two changes:

1. Move the WeChat contact information and business address higher on the website so customers can find them more easily.

2. Show four featured vehicles on the homepage at different rental price levels, from lower-priced to higher-priced options, so customers with different budgets can quickly see their choices.

## Changes Made

Based on his feedback, I added the WeChat information, business hours, and address near the top of the website.

I also updated the homepage to show four featured vehicles at different price levels:

- Li Auto L7 — ¥300–500/day
- ZEEKR 007GT — ¥350–400/day
- Xiaomi SU7 Max — ¥450–550/day
- Li Auto i8 — ¥650–800/day

These changes make the contact information easier to find and give customers a clearer range of rental choices.
