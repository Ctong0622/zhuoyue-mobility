## Client Brief

**Client:** Liming, who operates Zhuoyue Mobility, a car rental business in China.

**Purpose:** The website will help customers browse Zhuoyue Mobility's rental vehicles, see vehicle information, and submit a rental request online.

**Audience:** The main audience includes customers who need convenient vehicles for family travel and customers who need rental vehicles for business trips.

**Key action:** The main action is for a visitor to choose a vehicle and submit a rental request.

**Pages needed:**
- Home
- Cars
- Rent / Contact

**Content status:** The client already has the company logo, company information, business address, contact information, and real photos of the rental vehicles. The client can also provide vehicle models, rental prices, and availability information. Zhuoyue Mobility mainly offers EV and new-energy vehicles, including Li Auto i6, i8, and L7, Xiaomi SU7 Max and YU7, ZEEKR 007GT, ONVO L90, and other vehicles.

**Style preferences:** The client wants the website to be simple, clean, modern, and easy to read. Since Zhuoyue Mobility mainly rents EV and new-energy vehicles, the website should have a modern EV and technology-inspired appearance. The design should use clean colors and avoid an overly complicated interface.

**Inspiration sites:**
- eHi Car Services - The client liked how the rental information is presented clearly for Chinese customers, especially the pickup and return process. The website makes it easy to understand how to begin renting a vehicle.
- CAR Inc. - The client liked the clear focus on finding and renting vehicles and how the rental information is organized. However, the client wants Zhuoyue Mobility's website to have a simpler and less crowded interface.
- Hertz - The client liked the simple booking area and how customers can easily understand where to enter their pickup location, return location, and rental dates.
- SIXT - The client liked the modern and clean appearance, strong focus on vehicle images, simple navigation, and easy-to-understand rental process.

## Layout Plan

The website will have three main pages with the same navigation menu on each page: Home, Cars, and Rent / Contact.

The Home page will introduce Zhuoyue Mobility with a large hero section, a short introduction to the company, featured rental vehicles, and a clear button that allows visitors to browse the available cars.

The Cars page will display available rental vehicles in a simple grid layout. Each vehicle will have a real photo, model name, rental price, availability, basic vehicle information, and a button to start a rental request.

The Rent / Contact page will contain a rental request form. Customers will provide their phone number, ID information, driver's license information, selected vehicle, pickup location, drop-off location, pickup date and time, and return date and time.

After the customer submits a rental request, Zhuoyue Mobility will contact the customer by phone and then use WeChat to confirm the rental.

The overall layout will remain simple, modern, and easy to read on both desktop and mobile devices.
